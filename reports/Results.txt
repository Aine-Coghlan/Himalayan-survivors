ERROR ANALYSIS
In terms of the random forest model, it performs poorly in terms of correctly predicting that
someone died. There's still a bias within the model towards prediciting someone survived. This is
because there's an imbalance within the dataset. 98% of people survived so the model is more prone
to guessing a climber lived.
The confusion matrix shows this as the model has close to 100% success rate for true negatives (correctly 
guessed survival). But true positives (correctly guessed death) is 17%. If this was a model to be used in the
real world this wouldn't be ideal as, in this scenario, it'd be better to guess someone died and be incorrect than 
predict they survived and be incorrect.
